About Celesta

A student platform for astronomy and hands-on STEM.

Founded in 2025, Celesta began as a small student initiative and developed into a broader student-led platform combining scientific learning with technology, outreach, competitions, and collaborative projects.

Organization profile

Astronomy, technology and access.

Celesta is an astronomy-focused student organization built around astronomy education, research, technical projects, outreach, and hands-on STEM learning.

At its core, Celesta aims to make astronomy more approachable without reducing its intellectual depth.

Identity

Learning through practice

Students are encouraged to use data, software, research methods, and engineering skills to explore astronomy.

Work areas

Education, research, projects, and outreach.

  • Astronomy and astrophysics educational content
  • Research-oriented projects involving real scientific datasets
  • Technical and software-based STEM projects
  • Online workshops and guest sessions
  • Student competitions and hackathons
  • Mentorship, peer learning, internships, and digital outreach
